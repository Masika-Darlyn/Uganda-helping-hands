Uganda- Helping Hands is a community-driven organization based in Bundibugyo District, committed to protecting and empowering vulnerable children, women, and families living in conditions of extreme hardship. Founded out of lived experience, we provide safe shelter, education support, skills training, and economic empowerment to restore dignity, stability, and hope.

Our Story

Bukama Hakim, Founder & Chairperson

Sometimes the deepest compassion is born from the hardest experiences. I was born into a life that taught me very early what vulnerability feels like. When I was seven years old, my mother made the difficult decision to leave the marriage. That moment shaped my commitment to protect the vulnerable.

Every child deserves protection. Every girl deserves education. Every vulnerable person deserves dignity.
