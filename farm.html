Farming & Poultry Project

Empower rural communities through regenerative farming and improved poultry production. Create food security, income, and lasting change — one harvest at a time.

🐔 Join the Project Donate Now

From Soil to Sustenance

In Bundibugyo District, families face limited access to nutritious food and steady income. Our Farming & Poultry Project bridges that gap by introducing climate-smart agriculture, indigenous poultry rearing, and sustainable crop growing techniques.

Volunteers and local farmers work side-by-side — building nurseries, constructing chicken coops, learning organic pest control, and establishing egg production units. The result? Healthier families, economic resilience, and skills that last generations.

“Farming together means growing hope — not just crops.” — Masika, lead farmer

🐓 Poultry for Progress

Our improved local chicken breed project gives families protein-rich eggs and meat. Volunteers assist with:

  • ✔️ Construction of elevated coops (predator-proof)
  • ✔️ Vaccination campaigns & disease prevention
  • ✔️ Training on feeding using locally grown maize & millet
  • ✔️ Establishing egg hatchery micro-enterprises

🌾 Regenerative Agriculture

We work with farmers to restore soil fertility and increase yields without chemical inputs. Activities include:

  • ✔️ Building keyhole gardens & mulching techniques
  • ✔️ Drought-resistant crop introduction (sorghum, cassava)
  • ✔️ Farmer field schools & demonstration plots
  • ✔️ Composting and natural pest repellents

A Day on the Farm

🌅 6:30 AM – Morning chores — feeding poultry, egg collection, watering seedlings.
🌾 9:00 AM – Project work — constructing chicken runs, planting demo plots, training locals.
🥗 1:00 PM – Shared lunch — farm-fresh meal with the community.
📚 3:00 PM – Workshops — poultry health, organic farming, or business skills.
🔥 Evenings — cultural exchange, local storytelling, weekend farm tours.

Tangible, Lasting Impact

  • ✅ 10+ families now have protein-rich eggs daily
  • ✅ 12 new poultry coops constructed by volunteers & locals
  • ✅ Young farmers trained in regenerative agriculture
  • ✅ Women's cooperative sells eggs to local markets & schools
“My children now eat eggs every week, and I earn extra money — the poultry project changed our home.” — Grace, Bundibugyo
