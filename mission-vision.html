Mission & Vision

Guided by purpose, driven by compassion — our roadmap to transforming lives.

Join Our Mission →

Our Vision

A compassionate and empowered society where orphans, vulnerable children, and disadvantaged families live with dignity, security, and opportunity.

Our Mission

To support vulnerable communities through education support, humanitarian assistance, empowerment programs, and sustainable community development initiatives.

Strategic Objectives

To achieve our mission, Uganda- Helping Hands is committed to the following strategic objectives:

Safe Shelter

Complete the construction of a new orphan home providing safe, stable housing for orphaned and vulnerable children.

Education Access

Expand education support for vulnerable children through scholastic materials, tuition assistance, and school enrollment programs.

Women Empowerment

Strengthen women and girls' empowerment programs through skills training, mentorship, and economic opportunities.

Clean Water Access

Increase access to safe, clean water in rural communities through well construction and water projects.

Partnership Growth

Expand partnerships with local and international development organizations to strengthen program impact and sustainability.

Community Resilience

Support community savings and credit initiatives to promote economic self-reliance and resilience.

Our Core Values

These values guide everything we do and shape how we serve our communities:

Compassion

Serving with empathy, kindness, and genuine care for every individual we meet.

Integrity

Doing what is right, even when no one is watching. Honesty in all our actions.

Transparency

Open and accountable operations, ensuring donors and communities see our impact.

Community Empowerment

Building capacity from within, enabling communities to create lasting change.

Accountability

Responsible to those we serve, our donors, and our partners for every decision made.

Human Dignity

Respecting the inherent worth and value of every person regardless of their background.

Our Strategic Pillars

Our work is built on four foundational pillars that guide our programs and initiatives:

Child Protection & Care

Providing safe shelter, emotional support, and nurturing environments for orphaned and vulnerable children to thrive.

Education & Skills Development

Ensuring access to quality education and vocational training that empowers individuals to build better futures.

Economic Empowerment

Supporting women, youth, and families through skills training, microfinance, and livelihood opportunities.

Community Development

Implementing sustainable projects in water, sanitation, and infrastructure that strengthen community well-being.

The Founder's Vision

I believe deeply that human beings must help fellow human beings. Every child deserves protection. Every girl deserves education. Every vulnerable person deserves dignity. This belief is the heart of Uganda- Helping Hands, and step by step, together with compassionate friends around the world, we continue building a future where hope is stronger than hardship.
— Bukama Hakim, Founder & Chairperson

Bukama's personal journey from hardship to humanitarian leadership gives him a unique understanding of the challenges faced by vulnerable children and families. His vision for a Uganda where every child has a safe home, every girl can attend school, and every family has the opportunity to thrive continues to inspire and guide our organization's mission.
