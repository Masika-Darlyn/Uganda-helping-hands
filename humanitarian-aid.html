Humanitarian Aid

Extending a helping hand to vulnerable individuals and families in times of crisis.

Join Our Mission →

Responding to Immediate Needs with Compassion

In rural communities across Bundibugyo District, many families face daily struggles just to meet their most basic needs. Poverty, illness, natural disasters, and family emergencies can push already vulnerable households into crisis.

Uganda- Helping Hands provides emergency humanitarian assistance to vulnerable individuals and families when they need it most. Our aid programs offer immediate relief while also working toward long-term solutions that build resilience and self-sufficiency.

What We Provide

Clothing & Bedding

Providing essential clothing for children and adults, including school uniforms, everyday wear, and warm clothing for cold seasons. We also distribute bedding, blankets, and sleeping mats to families in need.

Shelter Support

Assisting families whose homes have been damaged by storms or natural disasters. We provide temporary shelter solutions and materials for home repairs when possible.

Household Necessities

Distributing essential household items including cooking utensils, hygiene supplies, soap, and basic furniture to families starting over after loss or displacement.

Food Assistance

Providing emergency food supplies to families facing hunger, especially during drought seasons, after crop failure, or during family crises.

Emergency Response

When Crisis Strikes, We're Ready to Act

When natural disasters, family emergencies, or unexpected crises occur, Uganda- Helping Hands mobilizes quickly to provide immediate relief. Our emergency response includes:

  • Rapid needs assessment to identify the most vulnerable
  • Emergency distribution of food, water, and essential supplies
  • Temporary shelter for displaced families
  • Referrals to healthcare services and other support
  • Follow-up support to help families recover and rebuild

Who We Help

Single Mothers

Women struggling to provide for their children with limited resources receive clothing, household items, and emergency support during difficult times.

Orphaned Children

Children who have lost parents receive clothing, bedding, and basic necessities to ensure their comfort and dignity.

Elderly & Disabled

Vulnerable elderly individuals and people with disabilities who lack family support receive essential aid and regular check-ins.

Displaced Families

Families who have lost their homes due to fire, storms, or conflict receive emergency shelter and household support.

Crisis-Affected Communities

Whole communities affected by drought, floods, or other disasters receive coordinated humanitarian assistance.

Terminally Ill Patients

Individuals facing life-limiting illnesses receive comfort items, bedding, and support for their families.

Our Distribution Process

Needs Assessment

We work with community leaders to identify the most vulnerable individuals and families who need assistance most urgently.

Dignity-Centered Approach

We distribute aid with respect and compassion, ensuring recipients are treated with dignity and allowed to choose items that meet their specific needs.

Follow-Up Support

We check in with families after distributions to ensure needs are met and connect them with other programs that can help them achieve long-term stability.

Community Involvement

Local volunteers help with distributions, ensuring transparency and building community ownership of the aid process.

Beyond Emergency Relief

While humanitarian aid provides immediate relief, our ultimate goal is to help families build resilience and self-sufficiency. We connect families receiving emergency assistance with our other programs:

  • Education support for children
  • Skills training for women
  • SACCO membership for economic empowerment
  • Orphan care programs for vulnerable children
  • Follow-up support for long-term stability

By addressing both immediate needs and long-term development, we help families not just survive, but thrive.
