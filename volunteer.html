Be the Change

At Uganda- Helping Hands, we believe that everyone has something valuable to contribute. Whether you have a few hours a week or can commit to a longer-term role, your time and talents can help transform the lives of vulnerable children, women, and families across Uganda.

Volunteers are the heart of our organization. They bring energy, expertise, and compassion to our programs, helping us reach more people and create deeper impact. Join our community of dedicated volunteers and experience the joy of making a real difference.

Volunteer Opportunities

Teaching & Mentoring

Help children with their studies, teach basic literacy and numeracy, or mentor young people in our education support program.

Flexible hours

Tailoring Instructor

Share your sewing skills with women in our empowerment program. Help train the next group of women in tailoring and business skills.

2-3 hours/week

Child Care Support

Spend time with children in our orphan care program — playing, reading, and providing the love and attention every child deserves.

Flexible hours

Construction & Maintenance

Help with the construction of our new orphan home, assist with repairs, or support maintenance projects at our facilities.

Weekends available

Photography & Media

Capture our work through photos and videos, help manage our social media, or assist with storytelling to share our impact.

Flexible hours

Fundraising & Events

Help organize fundraising events, assist with donor outreach, or support our community engagement activities.

Project-based

Why Volunteer With Us?

Make a Real Impact

Your time and skills directly contribute to improving the lives of vulnerable children and families in our community.

Join a Community

Connect with like-minded individuals who share your passion for service and social change.

Grow Your Skills

Gain valuable experience, develop new skills, and enhance your resume while making a difference.

Experience Joy

There's nothing quite like the feeling of knowing you've helped someone in need. Volunteers consistently report increased happiness and fulfillment.

What We Look For

Compassion

A genuine desire to help others and treat everyone with dignity and respect.

Reliability

Consistency and commitment to your volunteer role, even when things get challenging.

Open-Mindedness

Willingness to learn from and work alongside community members with diverse backgrounds.

Child Protection Awareness

Commitment to keeping children safe. All volunteers undergo a child protection orientation.
