Our Founder's Story

Bukama Hakim

Founder & Chairperson

My name is Bukama Hakim, founder of Uganda- Helping Hands. I was born into a life that taught me vulnerability firsthand. My mother, married at just 21, faced hardships in a polygamous household and later left to protect her children. By age seven, I knew what it felt like to be displaced, to struggle for stability, and to watch a mother fight tirelessly for her children's future.

I believe deeply that human beings must help fellow human beings. Every child deserves protection. Every girl deserves education. Every vulnerable person deserves dignity.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, I watched children in my village never return to school, young girls married off early, and families lose hope. I knew I had to act. Starting with a small gardening project, then a tailoring initiative with five sewing machines, Uganda- Helping Hands was born. Today, we care for orphaned children, provide skills training, and work to build a future where hope is stronger than hardship.

Our Commitment to Child Protection

Uganda- Helping Hands is deeply committed to protecting children from abuse, exploitation, and neglect. We operate under a comprehensive Child Protection and Safeguarding Policy that ensures the safety and well-being of every child under our care. All staff and volunteers undergo background checks and safeguarding training.

Our principles: Every child has the right to safety, education, and a nurturing environment where they can thrive.
